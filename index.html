FAQ

Questions, answered

When does the beta open?

Founding seats are filling now. You’ll get an email with access timing after you join the waitlist.

Do you replace Xactimate?

No. Estimating stays in Xactimate. Slatecliff runs the claim file, deadlines, and client communication around it.

What will it cost?

Founding beta locks intro pricing: solo from about $99/mo, firm plan around $249/mo for three seats. Final numbers confirmed before you pay anything.

Is my claim data safe?

Production accounts will use encrypted transport and isolated tenant data. The waitlist only collects email, firm, and pain — nothing about policyholders.
