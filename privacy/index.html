Privacy

Short version: SlateCliff doesn't ask for your personal information, doesn't use accounts, and doesn't run analytics.

What we collect

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement. We don't currently run analytics or set cookies of our own.

Affiliate tracking

Our pages include the Partnerize partner tag, which helps credit purchases you make through our merchant links. Partnerize and the merchants may set cookies or use similar technologies to do this. See Partnerize's privacy policy.

Newsletter

We don't collect newsletter sign-ups on the site yet. If you email us to join, we use your address only to send the newsletter, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

Merchant links

When you click through to a merchant, that merchant's own privacy policy applies, along with any affiliate network involved. Those parties may use cookies to credit a purchase.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Last updated October 12, 2026. Questions: hello@slatecliff.com.
