What stocking stuffers for him actually are (and what they are not)

A good stocking stuffer is a small, useful thing he'll keep using after the wrapping paper is gone: something for his keys, his coffee, or his gear. It should be cheap enough to tuck into a stocking but well made enough that he notices the difference.

It is not a gag gift, novelty socks, or a gadget that breaks by New Year's. Every pick here comes from a maker known for durable gear, and every one was under $30 when we checked.

How we ranked these gifts

We ranked each pick on four criteria. We don't publish ratings, review counts, or stats we haven't verified.

  • Type: What kind of item it is (for example drinkware, tool, apparel).
  • Price: The merchant's listed price on the date we checked.
  • Where he uses it: Home, on the go, or both.
  • How often: A rough call on whether it's an everyday item or an occasional one.
