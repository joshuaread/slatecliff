What a Black Friday deal actually is (and what it is not)

Black Friday is the day after U.S. Thanksgiving. In 2026 that's Friday, November 27. Cyber Monday follows on Monday, November 30. The four days together are often called BFCM, and many retailers run promotions across all four days. Whether a particular maker runs one, and on what, is up to that maker.

A real Black Friday deal is a lower price than the maker's regular price, shown on the maker's own site. The simplest test is to compare the sale price with the regular price we list here. We checked these prices on October 12 and again on October 26, 2026, well before any Black Friday promotions, so they're a clean baseline.

It is not a reason to buy something he won't use, and it's not a guarantee. A sale may cover only some items, or none at all. We won't list a discount we haven't seen. If a pick you like isn't on sale, it's still on this list because we'd recommend it at full price.

This list leans toward the bigger gifts, from $98 to $199.95, because that's where a percentage off saves the most. For smaller gifts, see our stocking stuffers under $30.
