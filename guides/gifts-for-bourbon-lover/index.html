What a bourbon lover's gift actually is (and what it is not)

A gift for a bourbon lover is something that makes the ritual around the drink better: how he carries it, pours it, shares it, and where he sits with it. It's for the guy whose shelf already has a favorite bottle and who doesn't need you guessing at a new one.

It is not another bottle of bourbon. Picking whiskey for a whiskey guy is risky: he has opinions, and a bottle is gone in a few weekends. It's also not novelty bar décor that ends up in a drawer. Every pick here is something he can use on a regular evening or a weekend away.

How we ranked these gifts

We ranked each pick on four criteria. We don't publish ratings, review counts, or stats we haven't verified.

  • Tie to the drink: Direct (used to carry, pour, or serve a drink) or setting (for the den, porch, or the evening around it).
  • Price: The merchant's listed price on the date we checked.
  • Where he uses it: Home, on the go, or both.
  • How often: A rough call on whether it's an every-week item or a special-occasion one.
