What leather anniversary gifts for him actually are (and what they are not)

The third anniversary is traditionally leather, so a leather anniversary gift is anything made of leather that marks the year. The best ones are things he'll carry or wear every day, so the leather wears in the way a relationship does.

It is not a leather trinket that sits on a shelf, and it doesn't have to be expensive. These picks run from a $30 sheath to a $198 splurge wallet, all from established leather makers.

How we ranked these gifts

We ranked each pick on four criteria. We don't publish ratings, review counts, or stats we haven't verified.

  • Type: What kind of item it is (for example drinkware, tool, apparel).
  • Price: The merchant's listed price on the date we checked.
  • Where he uses it: Home, on the go, or both.
  • How often: A rough call on whether it's an everyday item or an occasional one.
