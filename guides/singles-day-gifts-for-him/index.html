What a Singles Day deal actually is (and what it is not)

Singles Day (also called Double 11 or 11.11) is held on November 11. It started in China as an unofficial holiday for single people. Alibaba began using it as an online shopping day in 2009, and it has since become a major sale day there. In the U.S., it's up to each brand whether to run a sale on November 11.

A Singles Day deal is a real markdown from a maker's regular price, shown on the maker's own site, for a limited time. The useful way to judge one is simple: compare the sale price with the regular price we list here, and check the return window before you buy.

It is not a reason to buy something he won't use. Every pick on this list is a gift we'd recommend at full price, so a sale is a bonus rather than the point. It's also not a guaranteed event: none of the makers on this list had posted a Singles Day sale when we checked on October 26, 2026, and we won't list a discount we haven't seen.

If nothing is on sale on the 11th, you haven't lost anything. You've picked a gift early, before the Black Friday rush.
