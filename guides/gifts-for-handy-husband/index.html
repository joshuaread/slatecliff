What gifts for a handy husband actually are (and what they are not)

A gift for a handy husband is a tool or piece of workwear that helps him fix, build, or tinker. The best ones fill a gap: the tool he keeps borrowing or the pants he'd wear out on purpose.

It is not a giant tool set he doesn't have room for. These are single, trusted pieces from Leatherman, The James Brand, Filson, and Taylor Stitch.

How we ranked these gifts

We ranked each pick on four criteria. We don't publish ratings, review counts, or stats we haven't verified.

  • Type: What kind of item it is (for example drinkware, tool, apparel).
  • Price: The merchant's listed price on the date we checked.
  • Where he uses it: Home, on the go, or both.
  • How often: A rough call on whether it's an everyday item or an occasional one.
