What coffee gifts for dad actually are (and what they are not)

A coffee gift for dad is anything that makes his daily cup better: the beans, the grinder, the kettle, or what he drinks it from. The right one depends on how deep into coffee he already is.

It is not a novelty mug or a gift card he'll forget. Two picks here are manufacturer-refurbished units, which we label clearly, so check the merchant's refurb terms before you buy.

How we ranked these gifts

We ranked each pick on four criteria. We don't publish ratings, review counts, or stats we haven't verified.

  • Type: What kind of item it is (for example drinkware, tool, apparel).
  • Price: The merchant's listed price on the date we checked.
  • Where he uses it: Home, on the go, or both.
  • How often: A rough call on whether it's an everyday item or an occasional one.
