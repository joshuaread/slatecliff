About SlateCliff

SlateCliff is a small gift guide for women buying for the men in their lives: husbands, boyfriends, dads, fathers-in-law, brothers.

Most gift lists are endless roundups. We'd rather give you a short list of well-made things for one specific guy or one specific occasion, say plainly who each one suits, and send you straight to the maker.

How we choose

  • Every pick comes from an established merchant with reliable shipping. We don't list dropshipped items.
  • We favor things he'll use and keep over novelty gifts.
  • We checked every price and link on October 12, 2026. Prices change, so the merchant's checkout is always the final word.
  • Our descriptions are our own opinions about who a gift suits. We don't publish made-up reviews, ratings, or statistics.
  • When a product is refurbished, we say so.

How we make money

Some links may earn us a commission, at no extra cost to you. That never decides what goes on a list. See our affiliate disclosure.
